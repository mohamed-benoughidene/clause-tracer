Brightline Studio LtdDeveloper Agreement v3 · Confidential

Development Services Agreement

This Agreement is dated 5 November 2026.

Parties

(1)Brightline Studio Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales with company number 09876543, whose registered office is at 14 Harbour Street, Leeds LS1 4AB ("the Agency").
(2)Karim Benali of 27 Rue Didouche Mourad, Algiers 16000, Algeria ("the Developer").

Background

(A)The Agency designs and builds websites for its own customers and wishes to engage the Developer to carry out development work on one of those projects.
(B)The Developer is an independent contractor and agrees to provide the Services on the terms of this Agreement.

1. Definitions and interpretation

1.1In this Agreement the following words have these meanings:
"Charges" means the fees set out in Schedule 1.
"Deliverables" means everything the Developer hands over as part of the Services, including code, designs and documentation.
"Master Agreement" means the agreement between the Agency and its customer for the project.
"Milestone" means a stage of the Services listed in Schedule 1.
"Services" means the development work described in Schedule 1.
1.2Headings do not affect the meaning of this Agreement. Words in the singular include the plural.

2. Start and term

2.1This Agreement starts on the date at the top and continues until the Services are complete, unless it ends earlier under clause 12.
2.2This Agreement renews automatically for further 12-month periods unless either party gives 30 days' written notice before the end of the current period. mark every clause preferred
Brightline Studio LtdDeveloper Agreement v3 · Confidential

3. The Services

3.1The Developer will provide the Services with reasonable skill and care and in line with good industry practice.
3.2The Developer will make reasonable revisions to the Deliverables at the Agency's request.
3.3The Developer will use reasonable efforts to meet the Milestone dates in Schedule 1. Time is not of the essence.
3.4The Developer may decide how and where the Services are carried out, and may use their own equipment.

4. Changes and extra work

4.1Work outside Schedule 1 must be agreed in writing and will be paid at the hourly rate of £45.
4.2Either party may ask for a change to the Services. A change only takes effect when both parties agree it in writing.

5. Charges and payment

5.1The total Charges for the Services are £9,600, split by Milestone as set out in Schedule 1.
5.2The Agency will pay 25% of the total Charges on signing this Agreement.
5.3The Developer will invoice the Agency at accounts@brightline.example when each Milestone is delivered.
5.4Invoices will be paid within 60 days of the invoice date.
5.5The Agency will repay reasonable expenses that it has approved in writing before they are incurred, as set out in its expenses policy at https://docs.example.com/expenses-policy.
5.6All Charges are exclusive of VAT, which will be added where it applies.

6. The Agency's obligations

6.1The Agency will provide the content, logins and approvals the Developer reasonably needs, and will reply to questions within 3 Business Days.
Brightline Studio LtdDeveloper Agreement v3 · Confidential

7. Ownership of the work

7.1All work created under this Agreement is a work made for hire and belongs to the Agency from the moment it is created.
7.2The Developer waives any moral rights in the Deliverables to the extent allowed.
7.3Tools, code libraries and know-how the Developer owned before this Agreement stay the Developer's. The Agency may use any that are part of the Deliverables for the project.

8. Confidentiality

8.1Each party will keep the other's confidential information private and use it only for this Agreement.
8.2Clause 8.1 does not apply to information that is public, that the receiving party already knew, or that the law requires it to share.

9. Non-compete and non-solicitation

9.1For 12 months after this Agreement ends, the Developer will not provide similar services to any customer of the Agency.
9.2For 12 months after this Agreement ends, neither party will try to hire the other's staff.

10. Warranties

10.1The Developer confirms the Deliverables will be their own original work, except for third-party material the Agency has approved.
10.2No other promises are given about the Services except those written in this Agreement.

11. Liability

11.1The Developer's total liability is capped at the fees paid under this Agreement, except as set out in the Master Agreement.
11.2Neither party is liable for loss of profit or any indirect loss.
Brightline Studio LtdDeveloper Agreement v3 · Confidential

12. Ending this Agreement

12.1Either party may end this Agreement with 14 days' written notice.
12.2If the Agency ends this Agreement, no payment is due for work in progress.
12.3Either party may end this Agreement at once by written notice if the other seriously breaks it and does not fix the breach within 14 days of being asked to.
12.4When this Agreement ends, each party will return the other's materials and confidential information.

13. Data protection

13.1Each party will comply with data protection law when handling personal data under this Agreement.

14. General

14.1This Agreement is the whole agreement between the parties about the Services.
14.2If this Agreement and Schedule 1 conflict, this Agreement prevails.
14.3The Developer will not work for a direct competitor of the Agency for 6 to 9 months after this Agreement ends.
14.4Neither party may transfer this Agreement to anyone else without the other's written consent.
14.5Notices must be in writing and sent by email to the addresses each party gives the other.
14.6This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

Signed for and on behalf of Brightline Studio Ltd by Sarah Jones, Director

Signed by Karim Benali
